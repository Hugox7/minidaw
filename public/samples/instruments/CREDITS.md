The upstream source list identifies:
- Electric bass: Karoryfer — https://www.karoryfer.com/karoryfer-samples
- Acoustic guitar: Iowa — http://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/
- Trumpet and trombone: VSO2 — http://vis.versilstudios.net/vsco-community.html

Included selections:
- bass-electric: 16 MP3 recordings
- guitar-acoustic: 10 MP3 recordings
- trumpet: 11 MP3 recordings
- trombone: 17 MP3 recordings

The recordings are copied unchanged from the collection. miniDAW applies playback
gain and envelope settings and transposes the closest sample when needed. It does
not include the collection's JavaScript loader. No endorsement by the authors is
implied. Keep this attribution and license link when redistributing the samples.


## V1 beta 10 — additional piano and strings

Piano, violin, cello and bowed contrabass: Tonejs Instruments, prepared by
Nicholaus P. Brosowsky. Samples licensed CC BY 3.0 as declared by the collection:
https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments#license
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Original sources listed by the collection: piano, violin and contrabass —
VSCO 2 / Versilian Studios; cello — flcellogrl, Real Cello Notes (Freesound pack 12408).
https://freesound.org/people/flcellogrl/packs/12408/

New selections: 22 piano MP3s, 13 violin recordings, 13 cello recordings,
13 bowed contrabass recordings. Piano MP3s unchanged. Bowed recordings converted
to mono 44.1 kHz PCM WAV, trimmed, gain-matched and given a crossfaded sustain
loop by miniDAW. This is a lightweight single-dynamic-layer selection, not a
complete orchestral articulation library.

Pizzicato contrabass: 14 separate recordings from VSCO 2 Community Edition,
recorded by Sam Gossner & Simon Dalzell, sample cutting by Elan Hickler/Soundemote.
Source: https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
Source commit: 440300901dfe9275fd84e0b7763af1f8443ae62e
License: CC0 1.0 Universal, upstream LICENSE reproduced in VSCO-CC0.txt.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
These recordings were high-pass filtered at 28 Hz, gain-matched and converted from WAV to MP3 (quality 3). VSCO note filenames
use an octave convention one below scientific pitch notation; output files
are named for their sounding pitches (E0 upstream -> E1 in miniDAW).

Per-file source URLs, output paths and SHA-256 hashes: orchestra-manifest.json.
Playback envelopes and gain settings are applied by the app. No endorsement implied.
Keep the existing and new credits when redistributing the app and its recordings.
